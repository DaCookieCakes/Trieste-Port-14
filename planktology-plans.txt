Science now has a random open slot due to the removal of the chemistry lab. We gotta replace it with somethin'.

I'm thinking an ocean based research, similar to ss13 virology in a way. Collecting plankton and microorganisms from the water and doing research on them for science points. Their food, temperature, effects/behaviors and name are randomized. You get research points from growing them into larger cultures, by feeding them their preferred food and giving them optimal temperature before putting them in some kind of analyzer to get research points and log the species. They can also have unique effects of some kind, although i'm not sure what.


# Current loop

1. Plankton Harvester: pipe sea water in, insert a plankton container, turn it on. Filters out 3 colonies into the container, then shuts off.
2. Culture tank (UI): holds up to 3 colonies. Canister slot with dump/extract, beaker slot with +5u/+10u, lights, temp +/-10, radiation flash (cooldown).
   Research form per colony: guess the diet + up to 3 characteristics, X to rule things out. Notes are stored on the colony, so they follow it.
   Submit (alive, size 50+, linked R&D server): correct = research points (1000 + 750 per characteristic point), wrong = nothing. One submission per colony.
3. Plankton Separator: cryogenic filter. Deposit a container, everything freezes (no updates). Pick species out one at a time from the UI into the inserted container.

Sprites are placeholders (gas tank, freezer, water tank, cryo sleeper).

**Behaviors**

Aggressive: Kills other plankton in the same enclosure as itself **DONE**

Bioluminescent: Glows brightly

Parasitic: Touching a living being infests them with the species, causing bleed damage and draining hunger.

Radioactive: Spits out small doses of radiation

Charged: Handling their enclosure requires insulated gloves

Pyrotechnic: When put in a tank with other plankton, they explode as a self-defense mechanism.

Mimicry: Disguises very effectively as a defense. Harder to analyze, dish may even appear empty. Also makes it difficult to separate out from other species if they disguise as the other culture.

Chemical Production: This species has a byproduct of a specific chemical, useful for production if you get lucky.

Magnetic field: Interferes with electronic equipment nearby and Jellids.

Hallucinogenic: This species has a WILD high if consumed.

Pheromone glands: Alters the behavior of other species in its tank, either positively or negatively. Can be used to control certain unruly or dangerous species.

Polyp colony: Grows coral around itself at a rapid rate upon reproduction. Can quickly get out of hand if not contained properly.

Aerosol spores: Releases spores into a 3x3 grid around it. Anything not wearing internals will be infested with some sort of growth or cellular damage.

Hyper-exotic species: Species that have extremely unorthodox and strange mutations. Very hard to work with, worth a LOT of points if handled properly.

Sentience: A single organism gathered that exhibits behavior of an intelligent being. He wants the crabby patty formula.


# Objects

**Machines**

Plankton Harvester: Needs to be used in water and tries to gather 2-3 species of plankton with a cooldown. **DONE** (piped sea water, 3 colonies per run, shuts off after)

Plankton scanner: Lists species and the name, the size, and if they are alive and dead. Also the amount of dead plankton in the sample. **DONE**

Microanalyzer: Insert a culture of plankton to get a readout on different species, and obviously visible behaviors or shapes. Prints out a paper with information and leaves unknown features blank. Used for gathering extra information somehow.
Also, once a culture is grown and analyzed, it will convert them into research points for the RD server.

**Production**

Analysis ideas:

Certain things are triggered and marked down as discovered when they happen, more discoveries = more points

Guesswork: You must interact with the species in a variety of manners, putting guesses on their attributes and behaviors. If you get them all right upon final analysis, full points. Otherwise, the culture is not accepted to be analyzed again due to already being logged in the database (incorrectly).


**Other technologies (planktonics branch)**

Better research equipment:
- Genetic isolator: Allows for easier identification and separation of species
- Mutagenic stimulation apparatus: Forces mutations in the species with a lower chance of killing them
- Micro-vault: Allows for preserved storage of samples without needing food or other conditions.
- Microseekers: Small robots that can be deployed in containment units to locate, kill, separate, or identify specific mutations or body types.
